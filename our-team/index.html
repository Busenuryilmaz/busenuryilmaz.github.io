Chair

I’m Milo, chair 🪑 of this year’s committee. This means I everyone in the committee must do whatever I tell them to 😈, but also that you can blame me if this year’s BMN is shit. If, under some hypothetical situation, a global pandemic breaks out and we aren’t allowed to rehearse or perform, that’s not my fault though.
 
In all seriousness, I’m the person leading and responsible for the committee, and if you have any questions or feedback regarding the BMN, you can contact me through: milogermanus@a-eskwadraat.nl
 
I myself play bass guitar 🎸 (makes that sound that makes your head bop), with about 9 years of experience under my belt. I play pretty much all types of rock (classic, punk, metal, alt, post, etc.), with my favorite being alternative rock-ish music, but I listen to many more genres.

Secretary

Hi there, I’m Femke! This year I’ll be vice chair, which means I will help Milo manage everything and make sure he takes a break every once in a while. Luckily, most of my meetings with Milo are at the bouldering gym, which means he doesn’t need much convincing to stop working.
 
I also have the role as agent, so I’ll be responsible for the communication between the committee and the musicians and audience. At the BMN, I’ll be playing keys. I started playing classical piano when I was six and decided to participate in the BMN two years ago. This gave me the opportunity to explore different kinds of music and to discover the joy of playing with others.
 
Currently, I am in my second year of the master Theoretical Physics. During my free time I like making music (obviously), climbing, running, baking and crocheting. My hobbies change almost every week though, so by the time  you read this I might enjoy something else (except music, that one’s constant).
 
See you at the BMN!

Music is by far the most important thing in my life.
