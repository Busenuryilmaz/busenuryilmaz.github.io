The BMN is the UU Faculty of Science’s biggest yearly music spectacle at De Helling. Talented musicians from A-Eskwadraat and Sticky will ensure you have an unforgettable night filled with the most awesome musical performances. From K3 to King Gizzard and the Lizard Wizard, our setlist contains a huge variety of music, put together with the musicians you see on stage. Sounds cool? Come join us at the show!

The BMN isn’t only a fun show to attend, but also an awesome community to join as a musical member of A-Eskwadraat or Sticky! Throughout a large part of the year, we organize weekly rehearsals and other fun events. Here, you get to know fellow musicians, improve your musical skills as a member of multiple bands, and together with your bandmates, you work towards a professional show at De Helling. Do you want to be a part of the show? Sign up for the auditions! Want to know more about the workings of the BMN? Check out the wiki!
